# Risks and Deferred Work

- Runtime/browser infrastructure may be unavailable; report evidence gaps.
- Production migration, deployment, backup/restore, observability and operational readiness remain outside this campaign.
- GST statutory boundaries and accounting policy requiring professional confirmation remain open.
- Version 2 imports/exports, SEZ/UIN, RCM, e-invoice/e-way bill, returns, ITC reconciliation, credit/debit notes and multiple registrations remain deferred.
- Existing target-contract and credential safeguards must not be bypassed.
