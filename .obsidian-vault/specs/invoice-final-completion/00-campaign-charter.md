# Invoice Modernization Final Completion Charter

Campaign date: 2026-11-06 (IST)

This campaign integrates and verifies the approved invoice document lifecycle without accessing production, Live Supabase, shared databases, or real business documents. The denominator remains the existing 100-point invoice requirement matrix.

Success requires implementation, current-source static/test proof, disposable runtime proof, authenticated browser proof, and independent technical/security/financial acceptance. A missing gate is reported as incomplete rather than inferred closed.
