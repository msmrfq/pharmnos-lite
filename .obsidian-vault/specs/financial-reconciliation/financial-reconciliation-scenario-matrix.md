These are proposed future outcomes, not current application behavior. Amounts are INR. Each row is an independent scenario with its own initial state; entity IDs may be reused between rows because balances do not carry between scenarios. `Net AR = Gross AR - unapplied customer credit`; `Net AP = Gross AP - unapplied supplier advance`; `usable = payment amount - active allocations - completed refunds - other valid consumption`. Allocation reversal restores usable amount and unapplied credit; it does not also subtract a refund. Payment reversal consumes the remaining usable amount and reverses active allocations in the same transaction, so the same amount is not subtracted twice. For validation-only rows S-27 through S-30, gross exposure, unapplied amount, signed net position, invoice outstanding, payment amount, active allocation, usable amount, and refundable amount are explicitly not applicable before and after the rejected operation; no financial record is created.

## Required cross-scenario invariants

- Gross exposure, unapplied credit/advance, and signed net position are separate measures.
- Allocation reduces invoice outstanding and unapplied amount by the same amount; net position is unchanged.
- Refund of allocated money reverses allocation first; completed refund then reduces payment usability once.
- Allocation reversal restores active usable/unapplied amount and does not create a refund.
- Payment reversal reverses active allocations and consumes the payment once; it is not also counted as a completed refund.
- Payment usable amount and invoice outstanding never become negative.
- All future operations are tenant-scoped, atomic, idempotent, and auditable.
